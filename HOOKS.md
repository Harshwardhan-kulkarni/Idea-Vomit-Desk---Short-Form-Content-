# 40 hook templates

Five fill-in templates for each of eight hook types. Fill the [brackets] with your own details, then say it out loud: if it sounds like a template, rewrite it the way you would actually say it.

These are structures, not scripts. Idea Vomit Desk uses the same eight types when it suggests hooks.

## Contrarian

1. [Topic] isn't dead. It's just misunderstood.
2. [Common advice] is the worst advice in [niche] right now.
3. Never, ever, ever [common action].
4. You don't have a [problem] problem. You have a [real problem] problem.
5. [Popular thing] is telling you what you want to hear.

## Curiosity Gap

1. Don't say [common phrase]. Say [better phrase].
2. [Average people] [action]. [Top performers] [better action].
3. [Unlikely group] are better at [result] than [expected group].
4. There's a reason [successful people] never [common action].
5. The [thing] you ignore is why [problem] keeps happening.

## Story Lead-In

1. Not proud of it, but man enough to say it: [mistake].
2. I thought I'd be at [goal] by now.
3. I asked [person] what they'd do about [problem]. Their answer surprised me.
4. I almost quit [thing] right before it worked.
5. I did [action] every day for [time]. Here's what happened.

## Labeling

1. If you want [result], you're going to have to [contrarian action].
2. If you're not getting [result], you're probably missing this one thing.
3. If you [behavior], you're [label].
4. If you've tried [method] and it didn't work, check [overlooked step].
5. If you're still [habit], this is your sign to [action].

## Signaling

1. If you know what [niche term] means, this one's for you.
2. You know that feeling when [frustrating moment]?
3. You're not [bad label]. You just don't know how to [action].
4. "[Common excuse]." No. [Real reason].
5. Every [role] has a [thing] they're scared to [action].

## Framework

1. The [name] rule keeps your [team] from [problem].
2. [Number] things decide every [topic]: [list].
3. Run the [name] test before you [action].
4. The [number]-step [method] I use for [result].
5. Everything in [topic] comes down to [principle].

## Authority

1. I've helped [person] get [result]. Here's what actually worked.
2. After [time] in [niche], this is the one thing I'd keep.
3. I've studied [number] [examples]. The best ones share [pattern].
4. [Famous person] does [action] every [time]. Here's why.
5. [Expert] told me one thing that changed how I [action].

## Stakes

1. You're one [mistake] away from losing [result].
2. Don't [big decision] until you can answer these questions.
3. Here's how to absolutely ruin your [thing].
4. Never [risky action]. Here's why.
5. [Thing] is changing. The [audience] who adapt win.
